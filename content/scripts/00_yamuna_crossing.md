# Reel #01 — The Night of Birth: Yamuna Crossing

**Status:** Approved by Jagadeeswar (2026-09-08) — first production target  
**Format:** 9:16 · 20–30s · manual IG upload only after human approve  
**Quality bar:** Rajamouli *Varanasi* IMAX trailer energy — sacred, rain-soaked, epic scale. No cartoon / plastic / neon AI look.

## Story beats
1. **Dungeon** — Vasudeva cradles newborn Krishna (dark skin, soft divine aura, tiny anklets). Iron chains, torch smoke.
2. **Enchanted sleep** — Guards slump mid-stance; spears slip; cell door and palace gates open alone.
3. **Escape Mathura** — Barefoot through Kans’s wet empty streets; palace lamps distant.
4. **Yamuna bank** — Swollen monsoon river; Vasudeva hesitates, baby held to chest.
5. **The step** — Wades chest-deep; baby Krishna’s foot touches the Yamuna → river parts into glassy walls.
6. **Rain + Vasuki** — Torrential rain; Vasuki rises behind, multi-hood canopy over father and child as they cross.

## Master visual prompt
Cinematic Indian mythological epic, IMAX trailer grade, vertical 9:16. Midnight Mathura: Vasudeva, noble Yadava father in simple white dhoti soaked by rain, carrying newborn Baby Krishna wrapped in cloth against his chest. Photoreal, film lighting, wet stone, volumetric torch smoke, no modern objects. Sequence energy of S.S. Rajamouli Varanasi trailer: monumental scale, sacred dread, divine tenderness. Guards collapse into enchanted sleep; iron gates open alone. Vasudeva flees Kans’s kingdom through empty rainy streets. At the Yamuna, a roaring monsoon river; he wades in; infant Krishna’s tiny foot touches the water and the river splits into towering glassy walls. Torrential rain. The divine serpent Vasuki rises behind them, multi-hooded, protecting them like an umbrella of scales and gold as they cross. Ultra-detailed faces, realistic fabric and water physics, anamorphic bokeh, teal-and-gold color grade, no text, no watermark, no cartoon, no CGI plastic look.
